Fulfillment

From checkout to doorstep

Customers judge the business by how quickly and carefully an order arrives. The dashboard tracks every order through five stages so nothing quietly gets stuck.

The five stages

  1. Order placed – the order arrives from Shopify, Amazon or a wholesale invoice.
  2. Payment cleared – payment is confirmed before anything is packed.
  3. Picked & packed – the warehouse team prepares the box.
  4. Shipped – a tracking number is created and sent to the customer.
  5. Delivered – the carrier confirms delivery.

Catching late orders

Any order that sits in one stage longer than 48 hours is flagged in red on the home page. Each flagged order becomes a task in Asana for the person who owns that stage, so the problem is assigned instead of just noticed. The Integrations page explains how that connection works.
